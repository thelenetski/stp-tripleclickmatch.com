FAQ

  • Is it really free?

    Totally. Optional boosters and themes are available, but the core game never locks.

  • Can I play offline?

    Yes. It’s a perfect no-Wi-Fi travel companion.

  • What happens if the bar fills up?

    You can try again — or use a booster to clear space!

  • Is this for kids or adults?

    Both! It’s simple to play, but hard to master.

  • Can I compete with friends?

    Yes — leaderboards let you track who’s the true sorting master.
